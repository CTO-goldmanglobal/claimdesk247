# ClaimDesk 247 — Executive Summary
**AI legal receptionist & accident-intake engine · NSW · operated by Goldman Forge Legal**
*One-page overview for partners, insurers, and referral partners · 2026-06-14*

## What it is
ClaimDesk 247 takes a motor-accident report from the public, asks the right questions, and instantly gives the person **general guidance on how NSW road rules usually treat their type of accident** — then connects them to the firm. It is available **24/7** and keeps every detail **in Australia**.

## Why it's different
Most "AI" legal tools either guess (and can be wrong) or are just web forms. ClaimDesk is built the other way around:

- **A decision you can audit.** Fault guidance comes from a fixed, lawyer-reviewed rule set — not an AI making things up. Every step is logged and cannot be edited after the fact.
- **It never oversteps.** It gives a plain-language *band* (e.g. "this looks like a common pattern where the following driver is usually responsible"), **never a percentage and never a verdict**, always with a clear "this is general information, not legal advice" disclaimer.
- **A human is always in charge.** Anything serious — injuries, police involvement, requests for advice, multi-car, out-of-state — **stops automatically and goes to a lawyer.**
- **A lawyer signs off the law.** A qualified legal head approves the fault logic and wording before anything goes live — a simple yes/no, item by item.

## What it does for the firm
- Captures clean, structured accident intakes around the clock, with a ready-made summary and an internal brief for staff.
- Filters routine matters from the ones that need a lawyer's attention immediately.
- Produces a consistent, compliant, fully audit-trailed record of every interaction.

## Where it stands today (honest)
- **Live & tested:** deployed in Sydney; full intake → guidance → customer summary; independently re-tested (99/99 automated checks as of 2026-06-21; was 87/87 pre-T4/T6/T8, 79/79 pre-T6, 75/75 pre-MFA, 73/73 pre-Stage-2.5); bank-grade access controls, multi-factor login for staff, and an unchangeable audit log — all verified.
- **Coverage today:** the 6 most common NSW collision types (rear-end, give-way/intersection, roundabout, merge, reversing, multi-vehicle) — an estimated 70–85% of typical two-car moving collisions. A further 9 scenarios are drafted and ready to add.
- **Compliance posture:** designed around the **Privacy Act 1988** (consent-first, Australian-only data) and aligned to the **ISO/IEC 42001** AI-management standard (human oversight, transparency, lifecycle control). Formal documentation and certification are the next step, not yet complete.
