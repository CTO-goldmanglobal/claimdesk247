Evidence checklist

Alex (AI assistant)

Before the lawyer calls, please gather as many of these as you can. Each item helps the lawyer understand what happened.

  • Photos of all vehicles (front, rear, both sides, close-ups of damage)
  • Photos of the scene — road, signage, skid marks, weather
  • Other driver's name, rego, and insurer details
  • Police event number (if police attended)
  • Witness name(s) and contact details
  • Dashcam footage (yours or theirs)
  • Sketch of vehicle positions at the moment of impact
  • Any nearby CCTV that may have captured the incident
